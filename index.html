Find Your Methodology & Methods

A quick way to connect a research goal in rhetoric and writing studies to a research design — and to the specific readings from this course that support it.

Methodology the big-picture logic guiding your study — your orientation to knowledge, evidence, and the researcher's relationship to what's studied (e.g., ethnography, case study, institutional critique, grounded theory).

Method the specific technique(s) you use to actually gather or analyze data (e.g., interviewing, observation, coding, discourse analysis, archival document analysis).

One methodology usually draws on several methods, and the same method can serve many methodologies — interviewing shows up in ethnography, case study, grounded theory, and program evaluation alike. This tool asks about both, separately.
